📌Frequently Asked Questions

Q What is the minimum order quantity?▾
A: Our minimum order is 10 pieces per order. You can mix different models, brands, and grades in one order to reach the minimum.
Q Do you offer a warranty on used phones?▾
A: Yes. Every phone comes with a 30-day warranty covering non-user-caused damage. We test every unit before shipping to ensure it works properly.
Q Can I choose the color and version of the phones?▾
A: Orders ship with random color and random version by default. If you need a specific color or version, there is an additional fee of 50-200 RMB per unit depending on the model.
Q How do I pay for an order?▾
A: We accept bank transfer, Wise, USDT, and major payment methods agreed with our sales team. Contact us on WeChat or WhatsApp for the full payment guide.
